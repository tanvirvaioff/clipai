How it works

Three steps between a long video and a feed full of clips.

No editing skills needed. Upload, wait, download.

01

Drop the video

Upload a file or paste a YouTube link. Long-form podcasts, interviews, streams — up to 2 GB.

02

The AI hunts moments

Every segment is scored on hook, flow, value and trend. Below 75 gets thrown out. Only the best survive.

03

Clips come back ready

9:16 reframe, karaoke captions, headline overlay and a clean SRT per clip. Download MP4s and post.

Built for creators

Everything that decides whether a clip performs.

The details that separate a scroll-past from a share.

🎯

Face-tracked reframe

Smart detection with hysteresis, smoothing and scene-cut handling so the face never leaves the frame.

💬

Karaoke captions

Word-by-word tracking in six styles. Burn-in files fail loudly, never silently ship a captionless clip.

👥

Multi-speaker layouts

Single, 2-up split, 3-up and 4-up grids with aspect-correct panel crops so nobody gets stretched.

⚡

Harsh viral scoring

A 0-100 score with four sub-scores — hook, flow, value, trend — so you can see exactly why a moment was picked.

🔇

Silence trimming

Leading and trailing silence over 0.8s gets cut, so the hook lands on the first frame.

📱

Platform-ready output

1080 × 1920, H.264, AAC, accurate seeking. No A/V drift, no sub-HD delivery.
