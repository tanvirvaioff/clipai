01  ·  FEATURES

everything it does, on real clips.

upload a video once — transcription, viral scoring, face-tracked reframing and karaoke captions all happen automatically on your own server.

AI viral moment detection

Groq or OpenAI scores the transcript to find the 3 to 15 strongest moments. Anything under 75/100 gets thrown out — no manual scrubbing.

Smart 9:16 vertical cropping

MediaPipe face tracking with hysteresis, exponential smoothing and scene-cut detection. Single speaker, 2-up split, 3-up and 4-up grids.

Word-level karaoke subtitles

Whisper produces a timestamp for every word, burned in with FFmpeg in 6 styles — Hormozi, Beast, Leon, Minimal and more.

Hook text overlays

AI-written hook titles covering the first seconds — where a short-form viewer decides whether to stay.

Silence trimming

Leading and trailing silence over 0.8s gets cut automatically, so the hook lands on the very first frame.

Self-hosted & private

Run it with Docker on your own VPS. Your videos never leave your infrastructure. No watermark, no usage cap.

02  ·  HOW IT WORKS

three steps to viral clips.

from a 2-hour podcast to a feed full of shorts — without touching a timeline.

01

Drop the video

Upload a file or paste a YouTube link. Podcasts, interviews, streams.

02

AI hunts moments

Every segment is scored on hook, flow, value and trend. Below 75 gets thrown out.

03

03  ·  FAQ

questions, answered.

Is JpClips free?

Yes — 100%. It's self-hosted on your own server. You only bring a free Groq API key. No watermark, no minutes cap, no subscription.

How does it compare to Opus Clip?

Same core pipeline: AI moment detection, face-tracked 9:16 reframing, karaoke captions. JpClips is free and self-hosted; Opus Clip starts at $15/month.

Which AI providers are supported?

Groq (free), OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini — pick per job in the workflow settings.

What videos work best?

Talking-head content: podcasts, interviews, webinars, livestreams. 1–2 speakers give the cleanest reframes; groups get split/grid layouts.
